TAKANAWA GATEWAY CITY ／ TOKYO

A forest, 150 meters
above central Tokyo.

Eight minutes from Tokyo Station and seventeen from Haneda Airport. In March 2026, a new city was completed directly above the newest station on the JR Yamanote Line.
Art&Soul chose this city as an appointment-only meeting base—for reasons that go well beyond location.

ART&SOUL TAKANAWA GATEWAY MEETING BASE
WHY HERE

Where we meet shapes
what we are able to discuss.

This meeting base is available by appointment only.
It is not a permanent office with a dedicated reception desk. Your host will provide the meeting point when the appointment is confirmed.

Hard chairs make meetings end sooner. A window with no view keeps the conversation fixed on the numbers in front of us. A place quietly sets the boundaries of a conversation.

Our work often concerns the arc of a person's life: ten years from now, twenty years from now, and the future of a family. These are not conversations to rush. They need a place where no one has to hurry.

We chose Takanawa Gateway City because it offers not one fixed venue, but several settings to choose from, all connected from the station. A quiet floor for a complex discussion, a light-filled lounge for a first meeting, or a private room when documents need to be spread out. Within the same city, the setting can change to fit the conversation.

LUFTBAUM ／ NORTH 28-29F

For complex conversations,
meet on the garden floor.

At the top of NEWoMan Takanawa, about 150 meters above ground, more than 500 plants fill a floor of roughly 8,000 square meters. Tokyo's sky lies beyond the glass; soil and stone are underfoot. LUFTBAUM is open from 8:00 to 24:00, though individual gardens and venues have their own hours.

A stone table beneath the trees / Curved seating creates privacy
Best suited for
Conversations that should not be rushed: succession, business continuity, or family matters. Voices do not carry, and neighboring seats do not face one another.
Easy to reach
A public garden reached by elevator directly from the station, relaxed enough even for a first meeting.
Hours
LUFTBAUM is open from 8:00 to 24:00. Individual gardens and venues vary, so we confirm the latest information when arranging your visit.

JW MARRIOTT HOTEL TOKYO ／ 30F

A 30th-floor lounge
as a private reception room.

Opened in October 2025, JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo is the brand's first property in the Tokyo metropolitan area. Its lobby and lounge occupy the top floor. A large central fireplace is surrounded by individually arranged sofas. The quiet of a hotel is practical: the space remains composed even when the conversation pauses.

30th-floor lobby / Sculptural ceiling lights
Lounge / Generous spacing keeps conversations private
Open one screen and the table becomes a working meeting space
Corridor to the guest floors / An illuminated lattice wall
Best suited for
First meetings and welcoming a referred guest—moments when the setting should convey quality without an explanation.
For visiting clients
Daily limousine buses connect Narita Airport with Takanawa Gateway Station and the hotel. Haneda is about 15 minutes by Keikyu from Sengakuji Station, a two-minute walk away. Accommodation and meetings can be completed in the same building.
Conditions
Seating, hours, and use are subject to hotel lounge policies. We recommend the best setting at the time of booking, taking current occupancy into account.
For larger groups
The TAKANAWA GATEWAY Convention Center is located below the same building, including a roughly 1,640-square-meter hall for up to about 2,000 people—all indoors and directly connected to the station.
03
BUNKITSU TOKYO ／ NEWoMan TAKANAWA

Five meeting rooms
among nearly 100,000 books.

BUNKITSU is a bookstore with paid admission, and this flagship spans more than 3,300 square meters. Its lounge has 223 seats, with coffee and green tea included. Five meeting rooms for four to ten people can be rented by the hour. Spread out documents, share a screen, then return to a seat among the books.

Meeting room / Monitor provided
Meeting rooms
Five rooms for four to ten people. JPY 1,650–3,850 per hour depending on the room. Complimentary drinks and books from the collection remain available during use.
Admission
JPY 1,100 for 60 minutes, JPY 550 per additional 30 minutes, or JPY 3,850 for the day. Prices include tax and complimentary drinks. Corporate plans are also available.
Best suited for
Designing, comparing, and completing documents—meetings where people need to work with their hands. Even when waiting is unavoidable, guests have no shortage of things to explore.

THE CITY ITSELF

The meeting base is not one room.
It is the entire city.

Stretching roughly 1.3 kilometers north to south, the district was designed for a daytime population of about 100,000. It opened in phases from March 2025 and celebrated its grand opening on March 28, 2026. Everything useful before and after a meeting is connected indoors.

LiSH / NORTH 6–7F
JR East's experimental business-creation hub, available 24/7. It includes about 90 open seats, phone booths, meeting rooms for three to six people, and workshop rooms.
Takanawa SAUNAS / NORTH 5F
More than 1,600 square meters with nine sauna rooms across the men's and women's areas, plus coworking, teleconference booths, rental meeting rooms, and nap spaces. Open 11:00–26:00.
TAKANAWA GATEWAY Clinics
Located on floors 4–5 of THE LINKPILLAR 2, with internal medicine, health screening, comprehensive checkups, endoscopy, dentistry, and orthopedics. International patients are supported.

AFTER THE MEETING

No need to spend the final thirty minutes
searching for a restaurant.

With roughly 200 stores, NEWoMan Takanawa is the largest development in LUMINE's history. The garden floor alone offers more than 1,000 restaurant seats. There is no awkward gap after a meeting spent looking for somewhere to continue.
